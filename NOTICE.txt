Small LLM QA: Few-Shot Retrieval and QLoRA

This implementation uses pretrained Qwen3-1.7B and all-MiniLM-L6-v2. The
recorded experiment includes completed QLoRA adapter training and four final
inference conditions; it does not include pretraining a foundation model.

The public package contains source, tests, a clean notebook, synthetic demo
examples, and aggregate results. The original mixed dataset, test references,
per-example course predictions, model weights, and adapter weights are not
redistributed. Aggregate training and inference results describe a completed
Colab experiment, not a newly executed GPU run of this distribution.
