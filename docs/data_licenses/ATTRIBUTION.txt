Source attribution

Natural Questions / NQ-open: Google Research and collaborators.
https://github.com/google-research-datasets/natural-questions
https://huggingface.co/datasets/google-research-datasets/nq_open
Wikipedia-derived data: CC BY-SA 3.0; code: Apache 2.0.
Selected short questions/answers; normalized and regrouped for this project.

TriviaQA: Mandar Joshi, Eunsol Choi, Daniel Weld and Luke Zettlemoyer.
https://github.com/mandarjoshi90/triviaqa
https://huggingface.co/datasets/mandarjoshi/trivia_qa
Data and code: Apache 2.0.
Selected questions and answer aliases; filtered, deduplicated and regrouped.

SQuAD 2.0: Pranav Rajpurkar, Robin Jia, Percy Liang and collaborators.
https://rajpurkar.github.io/SQuAD-explorer/
Data: CC BY-SA 4.0. Original article titles and per-example Wikipedia URLs
are recorded in sources.csv. Questions and passages were selected by
length and answer validity, split by article, and paired with distractors.

HotpotQA: Zhilin Yang, Peng Qi, Saizheng Zhang, Yoshua Bengio,
William W. Cohen, Ruslan Salakhutdinov and Christopher D. Manning.
https://hotpotqa.github.io/
https://huggingface.co/datasets/hotpotqa/hotpot_qa
Data: CC BY-SA 4.0. Selected questions were regrouped; source passages were
reduced to two supporting passages and one distractor. Per-example
Wikipedia URLs are recorded in sources.csv.

GSM8K: OpenAI.
https://github.com/openai/grade-school-math
MIT License, copyright (c) 2021 OpenAI.
Selected short problems with 2-4 annotated calculations. Final answers
were extracted and reformatted; the original worked solutions are not
part of the student data.

SVAMP: Arkil Patel, Satwik Bhattamishra and Navin Goyal.
https://github.com/arkilpatel/SVAMP
MIT License, copyright (c) 2021 Arkil Patel.
Selected problems; body/question fields joined and numeric labels
converted to exact fractions. Questions were regrouped for this project.

Course-authored material
Computing/Python questions, fictitious policies and questions, new math
problems, and factual paraphrases/questions from official model sources
were prepared for this project. Original-source URLs for the factual
paraphrases are recorded in sources.csv. These course-authored additions
are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

The component licenses above remain applicable to their respective data.
Modifications and the combined course data compilation: CC BY-SA 4.0.
